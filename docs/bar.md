# The bar: reference mechanisms

What the founder liked about two reference sites, extracted as things a critic can check by looking. Captured 2026-10-06 from full-page screenshots at 390px and 1440px plus page text.

## Whoop (whoop.com): architecture, not colours

Liked for: the video hero giving way to a scrolling page, digestible sections with big titles and media, a call to action on each section, carousels for more information.

### Hero
- One full screen on phone (about 85% of viewport height below the sticky header), video background, pause button bottom right. Dark, warm, close-up footage of texture, no faces.
- Headline in the lower half, left-aligned, white, light weight, 3 lines on phone (~44px), 2 lines on desktop (~110px).
- One subtitle, 3 lines, ~17px, plus one small pill tag. No button inside the hero on phone; the first call to action is the card right after.
- Hard cut to white at the bottom of the video. No fade, no overlap.

### Section rhythm
- Every section: title, paragraph, media, call to action, in that order.
- Title: 2 or 3 lines, left-aligned, light weight, ~40px phone, ~96px desktop. Three type sizes on the whole page: title, body, button label.
- Paragraph: one only, 3 to 4 lines. Never two.
- Media: full width minus 22px gutters, 16 to 20px corners, always a photo or video of people.
- One thought per section. About 11 sections. Backgrounds alternate white, black, light grey; no divider lines.
- One typeface, two weights. Headlines never bold; the only bold is tiny labels.

### Calls to action
- Pills with uppercase, letter-spaced 12 to 13px labels. Filled black primary, outlined secondary. Exactly one primary per section.
- A reusable "get started" card (light grey, rounded, image, title, two lines, outlined button) appears twice on the page, same component both times.
- Final section before the footer is a single call to action with a product shot.

### Carousels
- Only where there's a list: features, pricing tiers, band styles, social proof.
- Cards about 85% of screen width on phone so the next peeks; dots below; one arrow overlaid on the right.
- Feature cards put the title on the image (top left, white) and a "+" bottom right for more. The carousel is the "more information" path; the section stays one thought without it.
- Social proof is a two-column masonry of photo and quote cards, not a carousel.

### Doesn't transfer
- Black/white/grey palette with colour only from photography (founder rejected the colours).
- Celebrity proof, three pricing tiers, pricing on the homepage.

## Headspace (headspace.com): header CTA, Q&A pop-up, colour

Liked for: the bright call-to-action button in the header; the question-style section whose rows open a colourful pop-up.

### Header call to action
- White header, three things on phone: small logo mark left, a filled bright pill ("Try for free") middle-right, burger right. The button is the only saturated colour in the header; ~40px tall, 15px semibold label.
- Same button on desktop, far right after the nav links. One button, never two.
- The button colour repeats in the closing call to action (blue pill on a yellow curved block) so top and bottom rhyme.
- Thin pink promo bar above the header, one underlined line.

### Q&A section
- Centered headline ("What kind of headspace are you looking for?"), 6 rows stacked on phone, 3×2 grid on desktop.
- Each row: white card, 1px light grey border, 12px corners, ~72px tall; question-style label left, a small coloured cartoon glyph right, then a chevron. Six rows, six colours; the glyph is the only colour on the card.
- Rows carry no answer text; the answer lives entirely in the pop-up.

### Colour without looking like a toy
- White page, single near-black type. Colour arrives as full-bleed blocks (one yellow card, one blue, one pink), each holding a whole section. Never two colour blocks adjacent; white between them.
- Inside a colour block the button inverts: black pill on yellow, white pill on blue. Still one primary per section.
- Cartoon elements are blobs with faces, flat colour, soft gradient, no outlines, placed at section edges peeking in around a headline, not centered as a hero.
- Big corners: 24px on section cards, full pill on every button and tab.
- One rounded sans, bold headlines, regular body. Headlines centered (unlike Whoop).

### Two patterns worth keeping
- Tabs plus card carousel: a row of pills, active one black, each tab showing a coloured card with title, 3 lines, button, illustration.
- A social-proof headline with four cartoon blobs floating around it.

## Where the references disagree (decided in brief.md)
- Headline alignment: left (Whoop) vs centered (Headspace). Decided: left.
- Headline weight: light (Whoop) vs bold (Headspace). Decided: bold.
- Colour: photography (Whoop) vs flat colour blocks (Headspace). Decided: flat colour blocks, cream/navy/sky/yellow.
